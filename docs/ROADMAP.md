# Functional Roadmap

## M1 — Local standalone foundation (`PASS`)

Supplier CRUD, evidence/rationale scoring, SQLite, truthful connector state, hidden start/stop, health contract, tests and AICompanyOS control-plane onboarding.

## M2 — Evidence provenance

Persist source URL/type, observation timestamp, reviewer, confidence, freshness and structured risk factors. Calculate deterministic explainable scores without paid AI.

## M3 — Governed AI enrichment

Add a provider-neutral enrichment interface, prompt/output schema validation, cost ceiling, retry limit and owner approval. Missing provider credentials remain `NOT_CONNECTED`.

## M4 — Review and reporting

Maker/checker separation, assessment history, comparison, export and independent QA. Procurement decisions remain human-owned.

## M5 — Controlled pilot and economics

Dogfood with consented non-sensitive supplier evidence, measure time saved and decision quality, and report `COLLECT_EVIDENCE` until real outcomes exist.
